# The Launch Feed — Brand Symbolism & Identity Guide

> **Tagline:** Ship. Vote. Rise.  
> **Brand Mark:** The 4-Square Stepped Telemetry Matrix

---

## 1. The Core Geometry: The 4-Square Matrix

The official mark of **The Launch Feed** consists of a balanced $2 \times 2$ grid with calculated mathematical opacity stepping (`100%`, `85%`, `65%`, `35%`) rendered in **Electric Signal Orange (`#FF4F00`)**.

```
┌─────────────┐ ┌─────────────┐
│  SQUARE 1   │ │  SQUARE 2   │
│   [ 100% ]  │ │   [ 85% ]   │
│   LAUNCH    │ │  COMMUNITY  │
└─────────────┘ └─────────────┘
┌─────────────┐ ┌─────────────┐
│  SQUARE 3   │ │  SQUARE 4   │
│   [ 65% ]   │ │   [ 35% ]   │
│ REVENUE     │ │   LEGACY    │
└─────────────┘ └─────────────┘
```

---

## 2. Symbolic Meaning of the 4 Squares

### 🟧 Square 1 — Top-Left (100% Solid Signal)
- **Concept:** **The Launch (Genesis & Ignition)**
- **Meaning:** Represents the exact moment a founder pushes code to production and submits to the queue. 100% solid, concentrated energy. It embodies the bias for shipping without hesitation.

### 🟧 Square 2 — Top-Right (85% Focused Signal)
- **Concept:** **Community & Peer Validation**
- **Meaning:** Represents the immediate feedback loop — fellow makers, early adopters, and voters rallying around the product through honest discussion, upvotes, and technical reviews.

### 🟧 Square 3 — Bottom-Left (65% Grounded Signal)
- **Concept:** **Verified Revenue & Transparency**
- **Meaning:** Represents cryptographic truth and real-world traction. Through direct Stripe and telemetry syncing, it separates hype from verified recurring revenue and sustainable business growth.

### 🟧 Square 4 — Bottom-Right (35% Ambient Signal)
- **Concept:** **The Leaderboard & Timeless Prestige**
- **Meaning:** Represents enduring legacy. Products do not vanish after 24 hours — they graduate into the Weekly, Monthly, and Annual Hall of Fame, establishing a permanent milestone for the maker's career.

---

## 3. The 4 Leaderboard Time Horizons

The four blocks also map directly to the four competitive timeframes of the platform:

| Block | Timeframe | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Top-Left (100%)** | **Daily Board** | Real-time launches competing during their 24-hour window. |
| **Top-Right (85%)** | **Weekly Board** | The week's top software tools filtered by sustained momentum. |
| **Bottom-Left (65%)** | **Monthly Board** | Highest verified MRR and compound growth leaders. |
| **Bottom-Right (35%)** | **Annual Hall of Fame** | All-time software champions archived permanently. |

## 4. Telemetry & Waveform Dynamics

- **Stepped Opacity Gradient:** Creates a radar-like signal pulse, visually conveying that a launch broadcast starts with high intensity at the core and radiates outward across the entire software ecosystem.
- **Brutalist Pixel Precision:** Clean right angles and crisp sub-pixel rendering (`shapeRendering="crispEdges"`) reject bloated corporate fluff in favor of high-performance developer ergonomics.

## 5. Official Color Palette

- **Electric Signal Orange (`#FF4F00`):** High-visibility action, urgency, and live launch broadcasts.
- **Obsidian Black (`#09090B` / `#000000`):** Terminal depth, focus, and clarity.
- **Pure White / Ink Light (`#EDEDED` / `#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast typography and laser-sharp readability.
- **Verified Emerald (`#00D97E`):** Cryptographic revenue and Stripe API telemetry proof.
